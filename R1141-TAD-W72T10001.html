R1141-TAD-W72T:10001

Briefing:

R1141-TAD-W72T:10001 is another networking error which follows the same resolution steps as R31A6-0-03 / R35A6-0-03 / S9041-TAD-W72T. Therefore, try the steps in that page first, as the section below only concerns players who live in Russia.

Note: If you live in Russia, especially the Russian Far East, or if your Internet Service Provider happens to be Rostelecom or smaller ISPs that rely on its infrastructure, and the steps in the R31A6-0-03 / R35A6-0-03 / S9041-TAD-W72T guide did not work, then you could try Yandex's Domain Name System (DNS). Players in Russia report that it may resolve this specific error. This only applies to players in Russia.

Privacy Trade-off: Yandex's DNS logs queries per Russian law, meaning they can see the domains you visit but not what you do inside, and may retain such information for some time. They may also be obligated to provide such queries to state agencies when requested. If you have been using your Internet Service Provider's DNS, then your queries are already being logged by them. What differs then is the retention period of your queries, that is, how long they are stored, and the degree of compliance and cooperation with state agencies.

On Windows

  • Win + I on keyboard
  • Click Network & Internet
  • Select your connection (Wi-Fi/Ethernet)
  • Scroll down to DNS server assignment
  • Click Edit
  • Change the dropdown menu from Automatic DHCP to Manual
  • Under IPv4: Enter the DNS addresses: 77.88.8.88 in "Preferred DNS," and 77.88.8.2 in "Alternate DNS"

On PlayStation (PS5/PS4)

  • Go to Settings, then Network
  • Select Set Up Internet Connection
  • Select your connection, press the start button while selecting it, then "Advanced Settings"
  • Find DNS Settings and change them to Manual
  • Enter the DNS addresses: 77.88.8.88 in Primary, and 77.88.8.2 in Secondary

On Xbox

  • Go to Settings, then General, then Network Settings
  • Go to Advanced Settings, DNS Setting
  • Change to Manual
  • Enter the DNS addresses: 77.88.8.88 in Primary, and 77.88.8.2 in Secondary
